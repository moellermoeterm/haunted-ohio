Room 214

A closed hotel. A floor plan with a missing room.

It is after dark and you are sixteen and bored. You slip into the old hotel on a dare, with a flashlight and a dying battery. Something walks the halls. Stay quiet, stay hidden, find the room that is not on the plan, and write down what you find.

Game material (fiction), for ages 14 to 18. Nothing here is graphic and nobody is hurt: if the Night Porter reaches you, he “checks you out” to your last checkpoint and you keep everything. Every sound has a caption, so it can be played on mute. Your name and writing stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.
