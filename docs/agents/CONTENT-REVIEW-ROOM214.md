# Content and safety review: Room 214

- **Ages 14 to 18, school-appropriate:** a quiet, dark hotel with a looming figure who never harms anyone. The "failure" is "checked out to the lobby/checkpoint", described politely ("Checking you out, guest."). No gore, injury, weapons, sexual content or real people. A test scans the game text for violent words.
- **No occult practice:** nothing is summoned or contacted; the player investigates and hides. The reveal (Room 214 holds the lost-and-found of guests, waiting to be returned) is gentle and hopeful.
- **Catholic-school context:** no religious content is needed or used; nothing conflicts with it.
- **Real history:** none. Hotels, towns, guests and objects are invented; any resemblance is coincidental. Nothing is labelled "Real history".
- **Fear without harm:** the tension comes from sound, darkness and being hunted by a harmless figure. Leaders may want to preview it for sensitive students.
