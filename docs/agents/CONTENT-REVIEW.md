# Content and safety review

Reviewer role: Content + Safety. Updated 2026-11-05.

- **School-appropriate:** a closed parish chapel, a Keeper who rang a bell on a bad night, and townspeople who were kept safe. No gore, no injury or death on screen, no sexual content, no real people. The tone is eerie but gentle; the "mystery" is that the township's notice hides an act of care.
- **No occult practice as gameplay:** there is no seance, Ouija, summoning or talking to spirits. The Echo is a replay the student watches; the figure "does not speak and does not look at you" and the clue text says "you are watching, not someone you are talking to". The story workshop prompt reminds students the strange thing is observed and investigated, never summoned.
- **Catholic-school context:** a chapel, a sacristy, saints' names for fictional chapels (Aurelian, Odile, Brigid, Colman, Aldric, Perpetua). Presented respectfully. A leader should confirm they are comfortable with the saints' names being used for fictional places.
- **Real history:** none. Nothing is labelled "Real history" and no clue contains a year. Every clue is tagged "Game material". Chapels, places, keepers and events are invented; the data file says any resemblance is coincidental. (An earlier WIP idea used a real regional flood year; it was dropped for this reason.)
- **No invented quotes presented as real:** all documents are in-fiction.
- **Student writing prompts** are skill-based (sensory detail, show-don't-tell, clue planting, tension) and keep stories school-appropriate.
