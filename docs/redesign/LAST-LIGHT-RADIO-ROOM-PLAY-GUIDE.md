# The Last Light: radio room play guide (one page)

This is the first redesigned scene. Only **The Signal** mission has changed how it looks. Its words, rules, costs, choices and saves are the same.

## The tour (new)
- The first time you start a new game on a device, three short cards appear. Swipe, use the arrow keys or tap to move through them, or press **Skip tour**.
- Then small bubbles point at what to do next, one at a time. Each one goes away when you do the thing it says. They use the words for the device you last used (keys, tap or click).
- The **?** button (where "How to play" used to be) lists one-line tips. Tap a tip to see its bubble again. **Replay tour** starts over; **Skip tour** turns the bubbles off on this device.
- In the radio room, five dots under the guide line show which step you are on, and a short label rises over the room at each milestone (Radio on, Signal locked, Analysis filed, Decision made, Mission complete).

## Getting there
1. Open The Last Light, choose **Enter the silence**, then **New game**, and build a survivor.
2. On the map choose the home settlement, then **Go to the radio room**.

## In the room
- **Move:** arrow keys or W A S D. On a touch screen use the stick (bottom left). With a mouse or trackpad, click or tap a spot to walk there.
- **Use:** stand next to something and press **E** or **Enter**, tap **Use**, or just click or tap the object.
- The line under the room always says what to do next. A gold outline and a prompt show what you are next to, so the dark never hides anything you need.
- **Esc** leaves a text box so the keys move you again.

## What to try, in order
1. Read the opening paragraph, then walk to the **receiver**. The mission panel opens beside the room. Choose **Power the radio and tune in**.
2. Tune with the **knob** (drag it), the **Turn left / Turn right** buttons, or the slider. Watch the needle on the receiver and the clarity bar; with sound on, the static fades and a tone rises. **Lock the signal.**
3. Some cases then have a decoding puzzle on the same panel. It is unchanged.
4. Pick the details that stand out, write your reasoning, and submit.
5. At the decision, walk to the **supply shelf** and use it. This is the existing "Check your supplies first" choice. Walk back to the receiver and decide.
6. Walk to the **notebook** on the cot and write the journal entry.
7. Leave by the **door**. You are back on the map.

Also look at: the battery crate, the window, the tape beside the dial, the headphones (after the radio is powered) and the card pinned above the bench. These only show a line of text. They change nothing.

## Settings (Save and settings, then "Radio room")
- **Text mode:** the original page-style mission, with no room. The game also switches to it by itself if the room cannot start.
- **Low effects:** even lighting, no flicker, dust or moving light. The game turns this on by itself if the device is slow, and when the device asks for reduced motion.
- **Brightness** and **Contrast:** three steps each.
- **Volume**, and the existing **Sound effects** switch. Every sound also appears as a caption at the top of the room.

## What to check on a real device
- Does it feel smooth while walking? (If not, does it settle after a few seconds, when low effects turns on by itself?)
- Is the dark too dark or too light in the corners?
- Can you do everything with the keyboard only, with touch only, and with a trackpad only?
- Are the prompt and the captions easy to read?
- Does a game you started before this change still open where you left it?
